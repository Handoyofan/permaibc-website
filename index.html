About

PERMAI-BC is a non-profit and non-political Indonesian community organization based in Vancouver, BC with missions to foster closer cooperation, to become a sourcing hub among Indonesians in British Columbia, and to introduce Indonesian arts and culture to Canadians in general.

Indonesia Fest

Indonesia Fest is an annual Indonesian festival hosted by the Consulate General of Indonesia in Vancouver and the Indonesian community in Vancouver, British Columbia. This collaboration was established in 2018 and a continuation of a long celebrated festivities by the local Indonesian community in Vancouver since 2015.

The community started to host Indonesian festival with the purpose to introduce more of Indonesia to Vancouver communities through our beautiful traditional dance performances, musical performances, delicious & rich cuisine, arts and crafts.

Join us at our events to experience the best Indonesian festivities in Canada.

This festival is an official collaboration work between the Indonesian Consulate General Office in Vancouver with the Indonesian Diaspora Community Group in British Columbia (PERMAI BC Association).

Kuliner Initiative

KULINER is an initiative started by the Consulate General of Indonesia in Vancouver and Indonesian Diaspora Community Group in Vancouver in 2019. This initiative is aiming to introduce Indonesian cuisine, with its richness in flavor and delicacy, to Vancouver food scene through variety of engaging dining experience as well as informative cooking workshops.
