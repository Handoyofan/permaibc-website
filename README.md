# PERMAI-BC Website

This repository contains the website for **PERMAI-BC** — *Persatuan Masyarakat Indonesia di British Columbia* (the Indonesian Community Association of British Columbia, Canada).

PERMAI-BC is a non-profit, non-political organization founded in 1995 and based in Vancouver, BC. It was formed by members of the local Indonesian, Dutch-Indonesian, and Canadian community with ties to Indonesia, to give Indonesians in BC a place to gather without political, ethnic, or religious boundaries, and to stay connected to Indonesian culture and heritage. Today an estimated 7,000 Indonesians live in BC, roughly half of them in Vancouver and the Lower Mainland.

The organization's mission is to unite and support the Indonesian community in BC by fostering inclusive social connections, preserving and promoting Indonesian arts and culture, and introducing that culture to Canadians more broadly. It runs community initiatives such as **Indonesia Fest** (an annual festival held in collaboration with the Consulate General of Indonesia in Vancouver) and **Kuliner** (an initiative introducing Indonesian cuisine through dining events and cooking workshops).

**Live site:** https://handoyofan.github.io/permaibc-website/

## What's in this repo

This is a static export of the PERMAI-BC website (originally built in Webflow), served directly via GitHub Pages — no build step, server, or database required.

- `index.html`, `about-us.html`, `articles.html`, `disclaimer.html`, `search.html` — main site pages
- `directory.html` + `directories/` — a community business/organization directory
- `directory-sub-categories/` — category pages for the directory
- `permaibc-news/` — individual news and event articles
- `css/`, `js/`, `fonts/`, `images/` — site assets
