Kuliner Bali

Food
May 26, 2019

Greetings,

On May 22nd, PERMAI BC and our Indonesian community groups in Vancouver, together as the KULINER Team, hosted a lunch series event with the theme of Balinese cuisine. We are grateful for the support from our community members and sponsors, with your help we sold out of the tickets for over 80 guests! The guests had the chance to have a Balinese dining experience and learn more about Indonesian cuisine.

The Kuliner Cooking Team with Chef Djaka (Refection Dining) leading this Kuliner lunch event, put together the Balinese lunch set menu. Moreover, our Balinese friends in Vancouver also participated as the greeters, servers, and the MC. It was as authentic as it can be.

Let’s watch the Balinese event highlight clip and don’t miss our next Kuliner event!

Regards,

Permai BC
