Pudjasera May 2023

Event & News
May 16, 2023

May 13 marked the highly anticipated return of Pudjasera, the beloved Indonesian food bazaar and community gathering in Vancouver, BC. The event exceeded all expectations, attracting a fantastic crowd of more than 85 enthusiastic guests.

A special thanks to the Indonesian Consulate General for supporting our event. Their unwavering commitment to promoting Indonesian culture and fostering community connections has played a vital role in the event's triumph.

We would also like to sincerely thank our esteemed food vendors: Peranakan Kitchen, Neng's Kitchen, Blueridge Treats, NH Nizar Taftazani, and Ambar Catering Java Food. Their culinary expertise and delectable offerings added a delightful array of flavors to Pudjasera, leaving everyone craving for more.

In a delightful addition to the event, Pudjasera welcomed a distinguished guest speaker from Visi8, a prominent media and entertainment Intellectual Property (IP) development company. With their expertise in transforming stories into various media platforms such as comic books, live-action movies, animated movies, and immersive experiences like Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR), Visi8 captivated the audience. Their collaboration with notable Japanese clients such as Netflix, Disney+, Warner Brothers, and Tojo Studio has firmly established them as leaders in their field.

We extend our heartfelt appreciation to all the guests who graced Pudjasera with their presence, creating an atmosphere of warmth, togetherness, and cultural celebration. Your enthusiasm and support are the driving forces behind the success of this event, and we are immensely grateful for your participation.

With the overwhelming response, we are thrilled to announce that Pudjasera will host more events throughout the year. Stay tuned as we continue to bring you the best of Indonesian cuisine and the vibrant spirit of community gatherings. Join us as we embark on this exciting culinary journey, celebrating the rich tapestry of flavors, traditions, and connections that Pudjasera has come to represent.
