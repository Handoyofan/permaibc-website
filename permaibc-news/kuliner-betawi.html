Kuliner Betawi

Food
April 1, 2019

‍

This was the KULINER’s first event that was held March 28th 2019 at the Holy Crab Restaurant. The Betawi Lunch was a huge success having over 100 people attending the lunch event from 12 to 1 pm. This Indonesian culinary promotion initiative is a collaboration between the Indonesian Consulate General of Republic of Indonesia in Vancouver (KJRI Vancouver) and PERMAI BC (Indonesian Community Association in BC) as well as Nusa Coffee, The Holy Crab Canada, and Tempea.

We are hoping to promote and introduce Indonesia through its cuisine in this KULINER Initiative. For more information and upcoming events, check out our website at www.kuliner.ca
